## Current behavior

- Construction and transport retain ground 0, above-ground 0–249, basements -1–-10, costed commands, physical journeys, elevator capacities/queues and safe route recovery. Camera/inspection/render pacing do not create simulation outcomes.
- Banks now coordinate cars in separate physical shafts, with shared stops/capacity/timing/access configuration. Oldest unassigned passengers receive a deterministic assignment and walk to that shaft before boarding. New/restored capacity redistributes stationary waiting calls once while retaining age/position; approaching walkers and riders stay committed. Disabling one car lands/unloads riders safely while others continue; unsafe active edits are rejected.
- The new normal-budget 20-floor fixture has 24 workers, two six-seat lower-bank cars, a walk across floor 9 and a six-seat upper car to floor 19, with no stairs bypass. Horizontal connectivity remains the existing continuous supported floor slab; there are no independently segmented corridors, wings or bridges. Public passengers cannot enter service-only banks; service staff may use public routes under the existing policy.
- Pure route/room diagnostics distinguish a missing entrance, disconnected geometry/stops, denied service access and temporarily unavailable transport. Person inspection exposes actual remaining legs/transfer progress. Completed wait statistics use the latest 2,048 boarding samples; current oldest queue age is separate. The pressure flag compares a bank/floor queue with that bank's currently available seats; it is not a proven sustained-overload rate.
- Office and residential contracts retain stable tenant/member IDs while people leave and return. Agreed rent, renewal, notice and physical departure are distinct from momentary occupancy. Prices and service/access affect demand and consequences.
- Cafe and shop customers accept a product, price and service duration, physically travel/queue, use staff slots and pay once after completed service. Applying another menu/offering resets only the future asking price; accepted orders stay fixed. Unpaid abandoned/closed purchases earn no revenue. Hotel booking/check-in/checkout/cleaning behavior remains integrated.
- Four original provisional offerings expose price, work-time and demand tradeoffs. The lunch modifier operates from 11:00 to 14:00. Base demand is shared per model, nominally every 600 seconds for Food and 900 seconds for Shop on the minute scheduler, with destinations weighted by demand/free capacity; terminal visitors are additional. Extra identical shops cannot each create another independent base stream.
- Explicit service jobs retain IDs, worker claims, routes, work deadlines and blocked/cancelled outcomes. Cleaning/repair costs post atomically; unsafe dismissal/demolition is rejected or waits for physical return. Histories are bounded.
- Manage exposes staff/tasks, demand/satisfaction contributors, resolving complaints and objective progress. Room controls distinguish physical capacity, contracted members and people currently inside. The HUD includes service staff in physical population; progression's peak population excludes them.
- Explicit quoted condo offers, owner/resident identities, ledger-linked purchase, recurring physical resident schedules, original-price buyback and evacuation before reuse now pass lifecycle/persistence/adapter acceptance. They create no lease or recurring condo rent.
- Session schema 7 contains transport schema 2, preserving individual cars/outages, assigned-car IDs and completed elevator legs alongside existing business state. Genuine schema-6 transfer fixtures and supported older schemas migrate to primary car 1 without rewriting committed finance/business state. All 63 persistence cases pass within current integrated verification.
- Food/shop controls display available offerings, suggested future price, actual accepted terms, current queue/service-slot use, retained recent purchase counts and lifetime operating ledger money. Shop shelving/checkout furnishings remain original procedural placeholders. Actual Godot controls, smoke and targeted graphical review pass.
- Thirteen dated location profiles retain independent network/site gates. The 2026-10-02 official-source audit is separate from unchanged release data pinned to 2026-09-30: ten qualifying networks corroborated, two documented exclusions and Abu Dhabi still Unknown/disabled. Docks stay optional, Hawaii-only and waterfront/rank-gated. Location demand inputs remain neutral provisional values; full external-arrival lifecycles are unfinished.
- Seventeen representative facilities and seven mixed-condition ranks remain original provisional tuning. The catalogue distinguishes verified management lifecycles, simple business prototypes and incomplete infrastructure. Tested category/unlock filters and the facility guide expose real costs, requirements and coverage. Registry validation rejects missing operating rules, model disagreement and nonfunctional occupant capacity. These do not replace the missing approved GDD, reference edition or exact 87-entry catalogue.
