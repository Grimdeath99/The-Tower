# Location and transport matrix

Audit checked **2026-10-02** for the advanced-transport request. The playable release remains pinned to **2026-09-30** in [locations.json](../Data/locations.json); this documentation audit does not change the data, its fingerprint, or existing saves. The game makes no network requests to decide eligibility. [LOCATION_SOURCES.md](LOCATION_SOURCES.md) retains the earlier snapshot record.

Ten profiles have corroborated qualifying subway infrastructure, two retain the documented exclusions below, and Abu Dhabi remains **Unknown**. This is not a claim that all thirteen locations or their terminal systems are complete. All parcels are fictional: an operating city network does not establish a connection to a particular building.

## Dates and eligibility rule

The checked date is when the source was consulted. The operating-status date below is either an explicit event date or an undated current passenger/service page observed during this audit. A current page does not certify that every station was open at an exact instant, and a historical opening report does not establish today's complete line inventory. No planned extension, network-size forecast or temporary service alert is used to expand eligibility.

A qualifying network has operating passenger railway infrastructure underground. Mixed surface/elevated/underground systems can qualify through that infrastructure. Elevated-only rail, a monorail, an airport people mover or road vehicles in tunnels does not qualify by name alone. Exclusion of the named systems is narrower than an exhaustive claim about every transport mode in the region.

## Shared permission and implementation matrix

| Infrastructure | Location/site/rank requirement in current data | Actual implementation | Missing acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pedestrian entrance and internal transport | All profiles; open ground-floor lobby plus actual permitted geometry | Authoritative journeys connect rooms, coordinated cars, real transfer walks, stairs and escalators. | `subway` | Eligible network **and** connected T site; basement footprint; geography minimum rank 1, plus facility/structural/operational gates | Validated location/site permission; bounded tourist attempts originate at the built terminal and must obtain a real journey to a destination. | No durable scheduled batch IDs, platform holding/throughput, deferred arrivals, shared allocation among external modes or terminal-specific outbound lifecycle. | `dock` | **Only `hawaii` + W site + normal rank 4**, ground-floor footprint and facility prerequisites; always optional | Same existing routed-tourist prototype, with strict Hawaii/waterfront gates. | No vessel-trip ID, onboard/disembarking ownership, berth/terminal capacity, boat schedule, outbound boarding or persisted boat queue. |
| Wings / skybridges | Approved concrete geometry and endpoints required; global floor convention/cap must remain | No corresponding facility IDs or complete structural-area model in the current 17-entry catalogue. | Stable area IDs, supported compatible endpoints, explicit connected walking geometry, safe demolition and access invalidation remain unimplemented. Missing approved details must not be filled with invented catalogue entries. |

No non-Hawaii profile may build a dock, including its W site. Hawaii C/T sites cannot build one either. The sandbox may relax rank only; it cannot change location, network, site or footprint requirements. Subway/dock remain optional and must not become universal promotion conditions.

The current location model stores identity, geographic scope, `SubwayNetwork`, fictional sites, neutral demand modifiers and source provenance independently. It does **not** yet provide a complete available-mode registry, per-location arrival schedules or regional capacity data. All four economic multipliers are explicitly `ProvisionalNeutral=1`; gameplay costs, demand cadences and rank gates are game policy, not researched local economic facts.

## Catalogue dependencies and current priority

The immediately blocked infrastructure definitions are **`parking`, `subway`, `dock`**. Their placements and simple routed prototypes do not meet the requested complete arrival lifecycles. `service-room` cleaning/repair and the operating `office`, `studio`, `hotel-room`, `condo`, `cafe` and `shop` all depend on valid internal routes; they are the real workloads for coordinated banks, transfers and service access. Existing `lobby`/`atrium` definitions are not permission to invent disconnected bank transfers or a skybridge.

The latest request prioritizes the missing bank/transfer foundation, then safe directional links/approved connectors, parking and eligible external terminals. Unfinished cinema, event and advertising lifecycles remain open business scope; this transport priority does not mark them completed. Complete progression/scenarios/incidents follows reliable transport and save/load, as requested.

## Reproducible geography checks

The [geography runner](../tests/VerticalDistrict.Geography.Tests/Program.cs) covers all thirteen profiles and site/rank combinations, unknown/ineligible networks, eligible city plus incompatible site, Hawaii waterfront versus inland, non-Hawaii waterfront and sandbox behavior. [Catalogue cases](../tests/VerticalDistrict.Management.Tests/CatalogueCases.cs) also exercise actual permitted/rejected placement and inspection purity. A test pass establishes the stored gates, not independently verified real-world infrastructure or complete terminal gameplay.

```powershell
dotnet run --project tests/VerticalDistrict.Geography.Tests -c Release
dotnet run --project tests/VerticalDistrict.Management.Tests -c Release -- "Catalogue:"
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\tools\verify.ps1
```

Current integrated verification passed all **22 geography cases within 260 total cases** and expanded Godot, following the 236-case pre-edit baseline. [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md) and [ADVANCED_TRANSPORT.md](ADVANCED_TRANSPORT.md) record the executed logs and limits. These results preserve the stored permission matrix; they do not convert unresolved real-world eligibility or unfinished terminal systems into verified features.
