# Migration fixtures

`schema3-office-night.json` is an unchanged schema-3 save from the earlier exported financial-management smoke, immediately before its first midnight.

`schema4-sold-condo.json` and `schema4-owned-condo-outside.json` were generated on 2026-10-01 with the unmodified, previously verified schema-4 core before ownership development. The preserved DLL SHA256 was `9C27D4B12E13F45A1602D92DAAC5987F999CAE391AADD40D673F9C30D3EACF8D`.

The fixture harness loaded the unchanged Data catalogues, created a sandbox session, built a ground lobby at x=0 and condo at x=8, and advanced ordinary logical ticks until a physical purchase completed. Ten more ticks allowed both original residents to enter. The first save is at tick 36,318, has two physical owners, and contains the original 2,200,000-minor-unit receipt. Advancing that same session for 31 days produced the second save at tick 2,714,718: the old thirty-day visitors had physically left, while the original sold flag and price remained. Both documents round-tripped byte-for-byte through that original loader before being copied into these fixtures. Their JSON was not edited into a newer or older format.

`schema4-free-condo.json` uses the same original DLL and geometry with the asking price set to zero before advancing. At tick 36,318 it has two physical owners and a sold flag, but no sale ledger row: the schema-4 admission path only posted positive sale charges. Its original thirty-day visit deadlines preserve the arrival time used to infer the migrated purchase timestamp. This third document also passed the original loader unchanged.

The schema-5 migration tests preserve historical cash and receipts, reconstruct missing household membership, preserve physical transport, and explicitly disclose adoption of the new 18:00-arrival / 08:00-departure residence schedule.

`schema5-mixed-retail.json` and `schema5-traveling-retail.json` were generated before the retail/product changes with the previously verified schema-5 core. Its frozen DLL SHA256 was `41CA303CCE30BE1A894B9D52D420554DE50AC5342345F046A1923C079F679A91`. The harness used the original rules bytes retained as `schema5-simulation.rules.json`, plus the unchanged construction and location catalogues. That rules fixture has no Products array.

The harness built the ordinary four-floor operating example in sandbox mode, added a shop at ground x=22 and a condo at floor 1 x=16, and advanced normal ticks. At tick 37,507 the first fixture has one serving café customer, an already paid shop visitor, 28 historical shop receipts, checked-in hotel guests, established rental households, and a completed condominium purchase. Continuing the same session to tick 38,100 produced a second fixture with a shop customer still physically arriving. Both complete documents passed byte-for-byte roundtrips through the genuine original schema-5 loader before writing; no versions, receipts, prices, or states were edited afterward.

Schema-6 tests retain old paid shop visits as completed purchases and turn old arriving shoppers into unpaid orders. Existing food prices and service deadlines remain unchanged. The historical rules hash is checked against the new rules with only Products removed; prior schema-2/3 rules may additionally omit the later Management block. Current schema-6 saves require the full current hash and explicit product/order fields. Tests also reject changed non-product balance rules, while preserving an already accepted old service duration when newly introduced product tuning differs.

`schema6-first-bank-riders.json` and `schema6-transfer-queue.json` were captured on 2026-10-02 before multi-car transport edits from the unchanged schema-6 core. The frozen DLL SHA256 was `53EA50ECCFAE230B89FD9E45065E6B410750B45D342ACCD8BF89FC702AF0197C`; that DLL and the three original Data files were copied to `.build/schema6-transport-baseline` before development. The independent capture harness references the copied DLL, not the current source project.

The paid sandbox fixture has floors 0–6, a ground lobby, three offices on floor 6, a two-seat bank at x=26 serving 0/3, and a one-seat bank at x=28 serving 3/6. The original route physically walks between shafts at floor 3. At tick 330 the first file contains 22 waiting passengers and two first-bank riders. At tick 353 the second contains 23 waiting passengers, including the transfer-floor queue, and one rider in the second bank. Both retain cash of 2,473,200,000 minor units and 12 ledger entries. Each complete save passed a byte-for-byte roundtrip through the original loader before being written; no version headers, passengers, routes, or receipts were edited.

Schema-7 tests migrate those documents to transport schema 2, retaining every original physical field, schedule, random state, and financial record. Each original bank receives primary car ID 1, existing waits/rides retain that assignment, and already completed elevator legs establish the initial completed-ride count. The rules and catalogue fingerprints do not change for this transport migration. Current saves require explicit per-car and dispatch fields; declaring a current multi-car document to be a historical version is rejected.
